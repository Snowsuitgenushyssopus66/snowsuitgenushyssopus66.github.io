🎯 About This Application

PRO-SHOOT acts as a coach for your basketball shot. It uses your computer camera to watch your shooting motion. The software identifies body parts like your elbows, knees, and wrists. It then provides feedback on your form. You can see where you need to adjust your movement to shoot the ball better.

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Graphics: A standard integrated or dedicated graphics card.
  • Camera: A webcam connected to your computer.
  • Memory: At least 4GB of RAM.
  • Storage: 500MB of free disk space.

📹 Using The Software

When you open the application, it will ask to access your camera. Grant this permission so the software can see you. Place your computer on a flat surface or a tripod. Step back so your entire body appears in the camera frame. When you take a shot, the screen will highlight your joints with colored lines. These lines show your shooting path. Use this data to compare your form with ideal shooting techniques. Focus on keeping your elbow tucked and your follow-through consistent.

🛠 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the software struggles to track your motion, check these items:

  • Ensure your room has bright, even lighting.
  • Wear contrasting clothing so the camera sees your limbs clearly.
  • Keep the camera lens clean.
  • Do not stand too close to the screen.

Can I use this with an external camera? Yes, the app detects any webcam connected through a USB port.
